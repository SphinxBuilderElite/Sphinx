| # | Stage | Gate to pass before moving on | Main source |

| 1 | Idea | Gap confirmed (overlap search done), FINER passed, PICO/PECO written | A, B §1, course S1 |

## Reconciled targets (where the parts differed)
- **Systematic reviews are reliable, not fast:** 10–18 months each (Part E cites a mean of ~67 weeks with a team of 5). Part B's 8–12 SRs over 2.5 years assumes a new one started every 6–8 weeks with a working team. Realistic plan: 6–8 projects in parallel at staggered stages, ≤4 led by Ahmed, ≤2 in writing at once.
- **ST3 scoring caps publication points:** best 2 first-author papers (4 pts) + 2 other (2 pts). FACT.1 Associate PI already fills one "other" slot. Minimum target is 2 first-author + 1 other; extra papers help the interview and lifelong CV.
- **30-month target (Part E):** 6–8 accepted papers by ~April 2029, ≥3 first-author, 2 national podium talks, a 6-month+ leadership role.
- **Excluded for ST3:** case reports, letters, technical tips, abstracts, Cureus (delisted from Web of Science 27 Oct 2025), group-name first authorship.
