## Where to start (if you only do three things this month)

1. Read *How to Read a Paper* chapters on the design you are using next.
2. Finish NIHR Learn GCP (if older than 2 years) and the first HealthyR chapters.
3. Bookmark EQUATOR, PROSPERO, the HRA tools and the BJJ author page. You will use them on every project.
